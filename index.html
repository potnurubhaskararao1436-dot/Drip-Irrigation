ESP32 Development Board

Microcontroller

Reads sensors and controls the relay module.

Connections: GPIO 34, 4, 27, 26, 21, 22
Power Supply: 5V DC / GND
Current Role: Executing core automatic irrigation algorithm
1 Sensor Sensing: Soil Moisture Sensor measures soil volumetric water content in Plant 1.
2 Climate Check: DHT22 captures ambient temperature & humidity data.
3 Water Level: Tank depth sensor monitors water availability.
4 ESP32 Processing: ESP32 ADC reads GPIO 34 (Soil) and GPIO 27 (Level).
5 Algorithmic Decision: If Soil < 40% AND Water > 10%, GPIO 26 triggers LOW/HIGH to energize relay.
6 Relay Switching: Relay closes 12V DC power loop to the water pump with total electrical isolation.
7 Pumping Action: 12V pump pressurizes water through filter & main PVC pipe.
8 Drip Irrigation: Water flows through branch pipes to 4 micro-drip emitters directly to plant roots.
9 Soil Moisture Rise: Water infiltrates ground, raising soil moisture & restoring plant health.
10 Auto Cutoff: Once moisture reaches ≥ 40%, ESP32 de-energizes relay, stopping pump.
